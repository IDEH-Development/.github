## 🧭 What We Do

IDEH works across four ecosystems:

| Ecosystem | Focus |
|---|---|
| 💻 **Digital Engineering** | Enterprise software, SaaS, AI systems, cloud, backend & APIs, automation, websites, mobile apps, UI/UX |
| 📈 **Business Growth** | Market research, competitor analysis, customer personas, branding, marketing strategy, CX, BI & analytics |
| ☕ **Hospitality** | Brand identity, packaging, digital experience, customer journey, POS, loyalty systems, QR experiences |
| 🏭 **Industrial Engineering** | Professional 3D printing, industrial manufacturing, reverse engineering, CAD design, rapid prototyping |
